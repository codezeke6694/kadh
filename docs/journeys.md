# Journeys

The flows every screen is walked and judged against before a task closes.

| id | who | their job | where it starts | steps | what shows when it is done |
|----|-----|-----------|-----------------|-------|----------------------------|
| J01 | new learner | choose which Kinnauri to learn on first visit | / | choose "Pahari Kinnauri"; tap "Start learning" | the home screen shows the word of the day in Pahari Kinnauri and the six games |
| J02 | learner | look up a word | /#/words | type "Cloud" in Search; open "Cloud" | the word page shows Cloud, बादल and its Hamskad, Navaskad and Pahari Kinnauri words |
| J03 | learner | browse words by topic | /#/words | tap "Animals" | the list shows only animal words, each with its meaning and the chosen Kinnauri word |
| J04 | learner | learn with flashcards | /#/play/flashcards | tap the card; tap "I knew it" | the card turns to show the meaning, and the next card appears with the count going up |
| J05 | learner | play pick the meaning | /#/play/pick-meaning | tap the first answer | the answer is marked right or wrong and the next question appears |
| J06 | learner | play match pairs | /#/play/match-pairs | tap the first word; tap the first meaning | the board shows matched pairs and a timer |
| J07 | learner | play memory flip | /#/play/memory-flip | tap the first card; tap the second card | the cards turn over and stay up when they match |
| J08 | learner | play word builder | /#/play/word-builder | tap the first syllable | the syllable moves into the answer row |
| J09 | learner | play true or false rush | /#/play/true-false | tap "True" | the answer is marked and the next pair appears with the timer running |
| J10 | learner | switch to Hindi buttons and another Kinnauri | /#/settings | choose "Hamskad"; tap "हिंदी" | the buttons read in Hindi and the games use Hamskad words |
| J11 | learner | see their progress | / | open "Progress" | it shows words learned, the day streak and best scores per game |
