# Journeys

The flows every screen is walked and judged against before a task closes.

| id | who | their job | where it starts | steps | what shows when it is done |
|----|-----|-----------|-----------------|-------|----------------------------|
| J01 | new learner | choose which Kinnauri to learn on first visit | /#/welcome | choose Pahari Kinnauri; tap "Start learning" | the home screen shows the word of the day in Pahari Kinnauri and the six games |
| J02 | learner | look up a word | /#/words | type "Cloud" in Search; open Cloud | the word page shows Cloud, बादल and its Hamskad, Navaskad and Pahari Kinnauri words |
| J03 | learner | browse words by topic | /#/words | tap "🐐 Animals" | the list shows only animal words, each with its meaning and the chosen Kinnauri word |
| J04 | learner | learn with flashcards | /#/play/flashcards/all | tap the card; tap "I knew it" | the card turns to show the meaning, and the next card appears with the count going up |
| J05 | learner | play pick the meaning | /#/play/pick-meaning/all | tap the first answer | the answer is marked right or wrong and the next question appears |
| J06 | learner | play match pairs | /#/play/match-pairs/all | tap the first word; tap the first meaning | the board shows matched pairs and a timer |
| J07 | learner | play memory flip | /#/play/memory-flip/all | tap the first card | the card turns over to show its word or meaning, with the move count beside the board |
| J08 | learner | play word builder | /#/play/word-builder/all | tap Place | the syllable moves into the answer row |
| J09 | learner | play true or false rush | /#/play/true-false/all | tap True | the answer is marked and the next pair appears with the timer running |
| J10 | learner | switch to Hindi buttons and another Kinnauri | /#/settings | choose Hamskad; tap "हिंदी"; tap English; choose Pahari Kinnauri | after "हिंदी" the buttons read in Hindi and Hamskad is chosen for the games; the last two steps put back English and Pahari Kinnauri |
| J11 | learner | see their progress | / | open "Progress" | it shows words learned, the day streak and best scores per game |
| J12 | learner | read one word on its own page | route: /#/word/cloud | look at the page | the page shows Cloud, बादल and the Hamskad, Navaskad and Pahari Kinnauri words, the chosen one first |
| J13 | learner | start the day on the home screen | / | look at the home screen | the home screen shows the word of the day in Pahari Kinnauri and the six games |
